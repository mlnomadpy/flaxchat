## Improvements to defer until their prerequisite is measured

Teacher distillation, mined-negative refresh, longer-context curricula, Matryoshka objectives and FSDP are proposed improvements rather than accepted capabilities. They should enter as isolated, immutable new stages initialized from the existing model, with a bounded TPU experiment and the same representative quality gates. Do not begin by replacing the stack or extending every scale campaign. Profile the qualified direct path first and select a measured bottleneck.

An optional experiment dashboard can improve navigation, but durable JSON/JUnit/GCS receipts remain necessary. A new paid dashboard or cluster orchestrator is not a prerequisite. A sparse/disk-backed preparation index becomes useful only when actual memory/scratch measurements justify it.
